# Atari Gamma Force: design

A demake of [MegaGammaForce](https://github.com/jasonfrowe/MegaGammaForce) (RP6502) for
the Atari 7800. The original is a *Radiant Silvergun*-style vertical
shooter: three guns that combine into seven attacks, a color chain,
weapons that level up, nine stages with multi-part bosses.

## What carries over

| MegaGammaForce | Atari Gamma Force |
|---|---|
| Three guns (A, B, C) combining into 7 attacks | **Two buttons**: button 1 = **Vulcan**, button 2 = **Radiant Sword** |
| Hyper Sword (Z+X+C with a full gauge) | Both buttons together when the gauge reads HYPER |
| Sword absorbs pink bullets, charges the gauge | Same |
| Weapon levels LV1-5 from experience | Same, for the Vulcan and the Sword |
| Chain: three kills of one color in a row | Same (red, blue, yellow; gray never chains) |
| Ship slows while firing / with the sword out | Same |
| 9 stages, each with a boss | Stage 1 first as a complete slice, then as many as the cart allows |
| Boss rush, extends at 50k/100k | Later |

The Vulcan is the strongest weapon and levels slowest; the sword is the
close-range answer and the only way to cancel bullets, so the two-button
version keeps the original's main decision: shoot from range, or close in
and cut.

## What gets smaller

The 7800 has a 1.79 MHz 6502 and MARIA draws a limited number of objects
per line, so:

| | MegaGammaForce | Gamma Force (target) |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy bullets | dozens | 16 |
| Enemies on screen | 16+ | 8 |
| Player shots | 8-16 | 6 Vulcan pairs |
| Sprite colors | 16 | 3 per sprite (8 palettes) |
| Playfield | 192x240 | 160x209 (160A pixels are 2:1, so the same shape art) |
| Boss | many-part sprite/tile hybrid | a few 16-line sprite strips per part |

Bullet patterns are thinned rather than removed: a 5-way fan becomes a
3-way, a 12-bullet ring becomes 6-8, spirals fire every other step.

## Look

160A mode. Art comes from MegaGammaForce's generated sheets, halved
horizontally (a 16x16 sprite becomes 8 pixels by 16 lines, the same shape
on a TV) and reduced to three colors per palette by `tools/gen_assets.py`.
Palettes:

- P0 sea, P1 deck, P2 deck detail (also gray enemies)
- P3 player and shots
- P4 red, P5 blue, P6 yellow enemies (the chain colors)
- P7 bullets: pink = color 1, blue = color 2, white = color 3; the sword

The background color is the open sea. Stage maps are regenerated by
MegaGammaForce's own map generator at 20 tiles wide (`tools/import_mgf.py`).

## Sound

POKEY at `$450` for music, TIA for sound effects, so music never drops out
when something explodes.

The soundtrack is MegaGammaForce's, converted: its music is OPL2 VGM at
60 Hz. A converter reads the OPL2 register writes, recovers each channel's
notes and volumes, and maps the 7 channels (lead, arp, bass, pad, kick,
snare, hat) onto POKEY's 4: bass on a 16-bit channel pair for correct low
pitches, the lead (or the arp when the lead rests) on an 8-bit channel,
and the drums on a noise channel. Notes are stored as events rather than
raw register writes, which would take ~40 KB per song.

## Cartridge

Starting at 48K while the engine settles; the full game will be a 128K or
256K SuperGame cart with POKEY, one stage per bank, code and sprites in
the fixed bank.
