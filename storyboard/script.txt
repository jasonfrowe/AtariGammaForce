# ATARI GAMMA FORCE: the in-game script (tools/gen_cutscenes.py reads it).
#
# A cutscene plays before each stage, and the epilogue after GAMMA PRIME:
#   == <scene> <kind> <picture or sector line>
#      scene 1-9: before that stage; 10: the epilogue
#      kind: "full" (a picture: storyboard/panels/<picture>.png) or
#            "briefing" (the speaker's portrait, and two header lines of
#            at most 14 characters:
#            after the kind, "SECTOR 2 | NEON SPIRES")
#   >> <speaker>: <callsign line>   starts a page; speaker is maya,
#      thorne or gamma (the briefings' portrait; ignored in full scenes)
#   then the page's text, wrapped to 36 columns (a blank line breaks it)
#
# Text is shown in capitals. Letters, digits, spaces and . , ! ? ' " - : /
# ( ) % only. A full scene's page holds 12 lines, a briefing's 15.
